# What to record

**Setup:** stop and start Play mode once, so the game reconnects to the restarted bridge. In the **Recorder** window, press **START RECORDING** before each drill and **STOP RECORDING** after its runs. One recording per drill.

**Getting to any drill:** Menu → **Practice** (or **Rehearse**) → pick the sample → **Keep Saltworks** → any agent → on the Weakness Report click **REHEARSE ›** on the only row → **BEGIN DRILL**. To replay it, use **Retry this drill** on the result screen. A red diamond on screen (the objective marker) shows where to go.

| Rec | Sample | Drill | Runs | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Weak entry** | Opening duel, North Causeway | 3–4 | First, hold on the **Weakness Report** for 5 s. **Runs 1–2:** run up the alley toward HELD ANGLE without aiming, and die. **Runs 3–4:** walk (Shift) with the crosshair on the marker, and win the duel. Then **Back to report** and hold 5 s. |
| 2 | **Weak clutch** | 1v2 post-plant, B site | 2–3 | Spike is already planted. Stay near DEFEND THE SPIKE, watch the front door and the back. One comes from each side. Keep the best run. |
| 3 | **Weak time pressure** | Late plant, C | 2 | Go immediately. Through the door, kill or dodge the anchor, **hold F** on PLANT ON C before the clock runs out. |
| 4 | **Weak smoke usage** | Smoke Kiln Gap | 2 | **Run 1:** cross the gap without smoking and get shot. **Run 2:** hold **C**, aim at SMOKE THE GAP, release, then cross. |
