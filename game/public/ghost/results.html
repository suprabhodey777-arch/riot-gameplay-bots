Transfer test

Chance for the guess test is 1 / (number of distinct ghosts seen, at least 2). The p-value is the one-sided exact binomial probability of at least this many correct guesses under chance. The transfer test compares each group's mean share of rounds won against the real player; "ghost minus default" is the difference of those group means.
